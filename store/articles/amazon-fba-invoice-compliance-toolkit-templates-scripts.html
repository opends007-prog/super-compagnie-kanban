Amazon FBA Invoice Compliance Toolkit: Fillable Templates and Supplier Scripts to Pass Account Verification

Amazon's account verification process for Brand Registry and standard seller accounts requires documentation that proves your supply chain. Sellers who source from retail stores, liquidation pallets, or informal wholesalers often fail this check because the paperwork does not meet Amazon's specific criteria. A single rejected invoice can stall a product launch or trigger a suspension.

This toolkit provides the exact structure Amazon expects, fillable templates for your records, and supplier communication scripts to obtain compliant documentation.

What Amazon Considers a Valid Invoice

Amazon evaluates invoices against three core criteria: the supplier's identity, the buyer's identity, and the transaction details. According to Amazon's Seller Central guidelines, an acceptable invoice must include the supplier's name, address, phone number, and website. It must also match the seller's registered business name and address. [1]

Amazon specifies that receipts from retail stores are generally not accepted as proof of supply chain sourcing for wholesale or brand authorization. [1] The document must be a formal invoice or purchase order issued by a manufacturer or distributor.

The Fillable Invoice Template

If your supplier provides incomplete receipts, you must request a revised document. Use this template to instruct your supplier on the exact layout required. Copy this structure into a word processor or spreadsheet and populate it with your transaction data.

Supplier Communication Scripts

Suppliers frequently push back on requests for formal invoices, especially if you are a small buyer. Use these scripts to frame your request as a standard accounting requirement rather than an Amazon-specific demand.

Script 1: Requesting a Revised Invoice

When a supplier sends a generic receipt or packing slip, respond with:

"Hi [Supplier Name],

Thank you for processing my recent order. I am integrating my inventory with our new accounting system, and my finance team requires a formal invoice to clear the payment.

Could you please issue a revised invoice that includes your company's full contact details (address, phone number, website, and tax ID), my business's billing address, and an itemized list of the products with quantities and unit prices?

I have attached a template for your convenience.

Thank you, [Your Name]"

Script 2: Requesting a Letter of Authorization

For Brand Registry, Amazon requires a Letter of Authorization (LOA) from the brand owner if you are not the manufacturer. [2] Use this script to request an LOA from your supplier.

"Hi [Supplier Name],

I am preparing to list the [Brand Name] products I purchased from you on Amazon. To ensure compliance with Amazon's Brand Registry, I need a Letter of Authorization from you confirming that I am an authorized reseller of your products.

Could you provide a signed letter on your company letterhead stating that [Your Business Name] is authorized to purchase and resell [Brand Name] products on Amazon?

I have attached a template for your reference.

Thank you, [Your Name]"

Script 3: Handling Retail Arbitrage Receipts

If you source from major retailers, Amazon may reject standard cash register receipts. You must obtain a "receipt" that functions like an invoice. Request a "Business Purchase Receipt" from the store's customer service desk or B2B portal. Ensure the receipt includes the store's corporate name, address, and your business's name. [1]

Maintaining a Compliant Documentation Trail

Keep a digital folder for every shipment. Store the invoice, the bill of lading, and any supplier correspondence. If Amazon requests documentation, you can export the entire folder as a PDF and submit it within 24 hours.

Common Rejection Reasons and Fixes

  1. Name Mismatch: The name on the invoice does not match your Amazon Seller Central registered entity name. Fix: Update your Amazon business name or request the supplier to reissue the invoice with the exact name.
  2. Missing Supplier Website: Amazon's automated system often flags invoices lacking a supplier URL. Fix: Add the supplier's website to your internal records and ensure it appears on the document. [1]
  3. Illegible Documents: Blurred or cut-off PDFs are auto-rejected. Fix: Request a digital PDF directly from the supplier. Do not photograph or scan paper invoices unless absolutely necessary.
    1. Retail Receipts: Cash register receipts from big-box stores are frequently rejected for wholesale verification. Fix: Use the store's B2B portal or request a formal invoice from the store's commercial desk.

Verifying Supplier Legitimacy

Before purchasing, verify the supplier's existence through public records. In the United States, you can search the Secretary of State business registry for the supplier's state. [3] This confirms the business is legally registered and active. If the supplier is a Chinese manufacturer, verify their business license number through the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System. [4] Amazon's verification team can cross-reference these databases.

Conclusion

Invoice compliance is a procedural hurdle, not a technical obstacle. By standardizing your supplier documentation using the templates above and communicating requirements clearly before you purchase, you can pass Amazon's account verification without delays. Maintain a clean paper trail from the manufacturer to your warehouse, and you will be prepared for any audit.
