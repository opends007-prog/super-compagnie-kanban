The Restricted Category Sourcing Playbook: SDS Templates & Ungating Workflows

Amazon gated over 20 product categories during the last major platform tightening, locking sellers out of high-margin niches like Health & Household, Beauty, and Grocery. Most sellers try to ungate these categories by submitting wholesale invoices, which works if you have established supplier relationships. But for categories requiring chemical compliance—topicals, cleaners, supplements—Amazon often demands a Safety Data Sheet (SDS) before they approve your selling privileges.

An SDS is a standardized document detailing chemical composition, hazards, and handling protocols. Amazon requires it to prove your product won't blow up in a warehouse or burn a customer's skin. If you source private label products or buy from domestic suppliers who don't normally provide SDS documents, you have to generate one yourself.

Here is the exact workflow for sourcing restricted products, generating compliant SDS documents, and submitting them through Amazon Vendor Central or Seller Central to get ungated.

Step 1: Identify the Gating Requirement

Before you spend money on documentation, verify what Amazon actually requires. Category restrictions vary by marketplace and change without notice.

Log into Seller Central and navigate to the "Add a Product" tool. Search for the ASIN you want to sell. If the category is gated, Amazon displays a "Listing limitations apply" or "Apply to Sell" button. Click it. The portal shows the exact requirements for that category at that moment.

For Health & Household and Beauty, Amazon typically asks for: - A 3-month invoice from a manufacturer or distributor - An SDS for the specific product formulation

If the portal only asks for invoices, do not waste time generating an SDS. Submit the invoices first. Only generate an SDS if the system explicitly requests one or if you are manufacturing a chemical product yourself.

Step 2: Source the Product and Secure the Invoice

Amazon requires invoices that meet specific criteria. If your invoice fails these checks, Amazon rejects your ungating request.

Invoice Requirements: - Issued within the last 365 days (180 days for some categories) - Quantity must be 10 units or more - Your supplier's name, address, and phone number must match public records - The product description must match the ASIN you are applying to sell

Do not use retail receipts. Amazon rejects invoices from big-box stores like Walmart or Target. You need a wholesale supplier or manufacturer invoice.

If you are buying private label products from Alibaba, ask the supplier to provide a commercial invoice with their registered business address and phone number. Amazon calls suppliers to verify inventory. If the phone number disconnects or the supplier denies selling to you, Amazon denies the application.

Step 3: Generate the SDS Document

If Amazon requires an SDS, you cannot hand-write one. The document must follow the Globally Harmonized System (GHS) format, which includes 16 specific sections covering chemical identification, hazard identification, and first-aid measures.

The Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA) enforces the Hazard Communication Standard, which mandates the 16-section SDS format for chemical products in the United States. You can read the exact structural requirements on the OSHA website: https://www.osha.gov/hazcom

How to Generate an SDS:

  1. Ask your supplier first. Many overseas manufacturers already have SDS documents for their chemical products. Ask them to send the SDS in English. If it exists, use it. Check the date. Amazon rejects SDS documents older than three years.

  2. Use an SDS generation service. If your supplier does not have one, use a service. UL Solutions offers SDS authoring services, and you can request a quote through their site: https://www.ul.com/services/safety-data-sheets-sds-authoring-and-management. These services cost between $100 and $500 per product, depending on the complexity of the formula.

  3. Use an SDS template tool. If you have the exact chemical composition and hazard data, you can fill out a template. The University of California provides a standardized SDS template that follows GHS guidelines: https://www.ehs.ucsf.edu/safety-data-sheet-template. You must fill out all 16 sections accurately. Do not guess the flash point or toxicity data. If you invent numbers and a customer has an allergic reaction, you face liability.

Step 4: Format the SDS for Amazon

Amazon does not accept SDS documents in every format. The document must be a PDF, clearly legible, and in English.

SDS Formatting Checklist: - 16 sections present and completed - Product name matches the ASIN exactly - Manufacturer name and address match the invoice - Emergency phone number included in Section 1 - Date of preparation or revision included - No watermarks or "draft" stamps

If you use a template, remove any university or third-party logos. Amazon reviewers reject documents that look borrowed. The SDS must look like it came from the manufacturer.

Step 5: Submit Through the Compliance Portal

Once you have the invoice and the SDS, submit them through the Seller Central portal.

  1. Go to Seller Central > Inventory > Add a Product.
  2. Click Apply to Sell.
  3. Upload the invoice and the SDS as separate PDF files.
  4. Do not combine them into one document. Amazon's system routes different document types to different review teams. If you combine them, the system might route the whole package to the invoice team, who will reject the SDS.
  5. Submit the application.

Amazon states that ungating requests take 2 to 4 business days to process. In practice, it can take up to 10 days. If you do not hear back after 7 days, open a case with Seller Support. Ask them to escalate to the Category Approval team.

Step 6: Handle Rejections

Amazon rejects most first-time applications. Do not re-upload the same documents. Read the rejection email. It tells you exactly why they denied the request.

Common Rejection Reasons: - Invoice not legible: Re-scan the invoice at 300 DPI and re-upload. - Supplier not verified: Amazon could not reach your supplier. Use a different supplier or ask your supplier to respond to the verification call. - SDS incomplete: A section is missing or blank. Review the 16 sections and fill in the gaps. - Product name mismatch: The name on the SDS does not match the ASIN. Update the SDS to match the Amazon listing title exactly.

If Amazon rejects your SDS because it lacks a specific certification—like an FDA registration for a topical product—you need a different document. The FDA maintains a database of registered facilities. You can verify if your manufacturer is registered by searching the FDA Registration and Listing Database: https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfRL/rl.cfm

If your manufacturer is not registered, you cannot sell the product in gated categories that require FDA compliance. You must find a new supplier.

Step 7: Maintain Compliance After Ungating

Getting ungated is not permanent. Amazon re-audits gated categories. If you change suppliers, reformulate a product, or let your SDS expire, Amazon can re-gate your account.

Keep a compliance folder for every gated product. Store the current invoice, the SDS, and any lab test results. When Amazon requests an update, you can submit the documents within an hour instead of scrambling to generate them from scratch.

This workflow applies to any restricted chemical category. The specific requirements change, but the process remains the same: verify the requirement, source the product, generate the document, and submit it cleanly.
